## The ruling

The 56% figure is **reference-page shortlist recall**. It is not research
coverage and it is not decision accuracy. Every reported number carries the label
of what was actually measured.

A number that averages over two different facts is split and both are reported.

## Why

This project has produced three misleading numbers, each confident and each
wrong in the same way:

- "78% coverage" was a keyword-match rate, not coverage.
- "discovery recall 47%" conflated never-retrieved with retrieved-then-reduced.
  Split, it was retrieval 67% and shortlist 47%.
- A sitemap-children hypothesis was asserted to "recover most of the fourteen".
  Verified, it recovered none.

An unqualified number gets believed, and then gets built on.

## Test of compliance

If the label and the measurement are not the same thing, the label is wrong —
regardless of whether the number is right.
