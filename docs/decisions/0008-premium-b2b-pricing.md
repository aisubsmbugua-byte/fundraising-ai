# 0008 — Premium B2B pricing: the schedule, the band, the founding terms

**Date:** 2026-09-22
**Status:** **Decided by the owner** on the parts marked owner; decision-space
proposals within the owner's stated band on the parts marked proposed.
Extends decision 0006 (credits are the unit); supersedes nothing.

## Adopted by the owner

1. **The per-operation credit schedule** from review 0022, as proposed:
   research 25 · strategy 15 · discovery 10 · proposal 5 · verification 2 ·
   small drafts and suggestions 1 · **failed, empty or platform-killed runs
   0** (now policy, not recommendation). Provisional rows re-price from
   ledger data as it accumulates; every price keeps its basis (ruling 0021).

2. **Positioning: premium B2B.** Annual subscriptions between **$5,000 and
   $10,000**. Target customer: organizations trying to raise **$200,000 or
   more**. The value anchor is the campaign, not the token: at the band's
   ends the product costs 2.5–5% of a single campaign's floor, and one won
   grant pays for it several times over. Credits therefore function as
   generous fair-use metering — accounting truth, not daily experience
   (decision 0006) — and are sized so no serious team ever rations.

3. **Founding-tester terms, tightened by the owner:** 30 days free at 1,000
   credits/month, in exchange for a weekly feedback conversation (or
   written notes), run-ledger usage observation, and a case-study option;
   then a founding rate of 50% off year one. Maximum two organizations on
   these terms.

4. **The nightly discovery cron is paused while testers are paused**
   (item 64) — background spend with no reader.

## Proposed within the band (owner to confirm shapes)

- **Core — $5,000/year**: 2,500 credits/month, 3 seats.
- **Premium — $10,000/year**: 6,000 credits/month, 8 seats, priority
  support.
- Top-up pricing deferred: at these allowances a team hitting the ceiling
  is a conversation, not a checkout.

Margin sanity: full Core consumption spent entirely on research (the most
expensive measured operation) is ~100 runs ≈ $65/month of model cost against
$417/month of revenue; the schedule's margins hold by an order of magnitude
at premium pricing, and the binding constraint becomes value delivered, not
cost recovered.

## What follows

- Item 25 narrows to: re-price the provisional schedule rows from ledger
  data before the first invoice; confirm the two plan shapes above.
- Billing enforcement still waits on the dead-end guard precondition
  (decision 0004 §3, carried through 0006) — at 0-credit failed runs, every
  wasted run is the company's margin, which is the correct pressure.

## Revisit

When the first paying contract is drafted, or if founding testers' measured
usage says the allowances are mis-sized.
