# 0007 — The pathway to the live product, compressed to Saturday

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Status:** **Decided by the owner.** Direction, mutable. The promise below is
the owner's, verbatim in substance; the sequencing and scope lines are the
decision space's recommendation, accepted by the owner with the deadline
2026-09-26 (Saturday).

## The promise

Intelligent and consistent donor finding · AI-powered fundraising strategy ·
AI-powered outreach strategy using the path of least resistance through the
user's personal network · grant proposals and pitch-deck design · email
sending with user approval · CRM with stages · nurture strategy and nurture
communication · a monetization model (what a subscription covers, how testers
are induced).

## Where each element stands (verified against the code 2026-09-21)

- **CRM + stages:** done and live.
- **Fundraising strategy:** live (auto-run on acceptance, human-approved);
  one display gap — 8 strategy facts absent from the research screen (item 26).
- **Donor finding:** live; consistency ceiling measured and named (ruling
  0025 — name-silent pages). Fix designed, parked behind pricing by the
  owner's own ordering.
- **Drafting:** live for outreach; proposals designed in the workflow but
  unbuilt; deck design net-new.
- **Email send:** Slice 8 spec exists with guardrails written; zero code.
- **Nurture:** the explicitly not-yet-designed slice.
- **Network outreach:** nothing exists; most novel, most privacy-sensitive.
- **Monetization:** credits-per-operation decided (decision 0006); run ledger
  live and metering since 2026-09-21; schedule, tiers and trial terms open.

## The order, and why

1. **Close the loop (Slice 8).** Find → strategize → draft → send-with-
   approval is the minimum honest version of the promise. Gated on the item
   21 ruling: the no-auto-send guarantee moves from true-by-absence into
   construction before any send code exists.
2. **Strategy display completeness (item 26).**
3. **Proposals, then a v1 deck** rendered from approved strategy + evidence.
4. **Nurture v1:** AI-suggested cadences, human-approved drafts, delivered
   only through Slice 8's gate.
5. **Network outreach v1 — manual capture only:** the user records their own
   network; AI maps which contact plausibly opens which funder door. **No
   automatic import of anyone's contacts or social graph** — that requires
   its own decision cycle (source, consent, storage) and is explicitly out
   of the Saturday scope.
6. **Monetization in parallel:** provisional credit schedule from measured
   operation costs (the ledger records them now), tier structure (a
   subscription = credit allowance + seats), founding-tester terms (free
   credits for structured feedback).
7. **Donor-finding consistency:** the ruling-0025-clause-4 experiment runs
   once the provisional schedule exists.

## What Saturday explicitly does NOT include

Full landing of the evidence-first qualification pipeline (6 of its 10 steps
remain; weeks, not days); automatic network imports; bulk or scheduled
sending of any kind (never, per hard rule 1); polished deck templating;
battle-tested pricing. Each ships as a v1 with its depth named, or not at
all — a partial close ships and is labelled partial (ruling 0013).

## Revisit

At Saturday's close, or the moment tester feedback contradicts the order.
