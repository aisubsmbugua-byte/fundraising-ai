# Importing people into your Network, and how a large network is searched

This is a plain-language note for the people who use the Network page. It is not a ruling; the rules behind it are ruling 0040.

## Adding people

You can add people one at a time on the Network page, or import a file of people you already hold: a donor list, a board list, a spreadsheet exported from any service. Save the file as CSV and choose "Import people from a file".

What is kept about each person is only: a name, where they work or serve, how you know them, how close you are (close, warm or acquaintance), and any notes. Nothing else from your file is kept.

**Email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, website links and social handles are never stored.** A column whose heading looks like one of those cannot be used for anything, not even Notes, and the page tells you so. The page looks at headings only. If you paste an email address into a notes cell yourself, it will be kept as notes, so keep sensitive details out of notes.

## How the import works

1. Choose the file. It is read in your browser; nothing is sent until you press Import.
2. Say which column is which. The page suggests a column only when its heading exactly matches a common name (for example "Company" for where they work); you can change every suggestion, and with no match nothing is chosen for you.
3. Choose how close you are to these people. This applies to everyone in the file. There is no default.
4. Say how you know them: a column, or one sentence you type (for example "From our 2024 donor list"). That sentence is how the origin of the batch is recorded, because the file's name is not kept.
5. Look at the list of columns that will NOT be imported, the preview of the first five people, and the counts: rows read, people to import, people already on your Network (same name and affiliation) that will be skipped, repeats within the file, rows without a name, and rows with a field too long. A role or title is joined to the affiliation as "Role, Affiliation".

Duplicates are found only by exact name and affiliation (ignoring capital letters and extra spaces). "Jon Smith" and "John Smith" are treated as different people. One import takes at most 5,000 people; split a longer file into parts.

If an import was a mistake, select the people on the Network page and remove them. The page asks you to confirm first and says how many will go. Removing a person also removes any introduction paths that were suggested through them.

## Searching a long list

The Network page shows 50 people at a time. Search looks for your words in a person's name or where they work. "Select all" can select everyone who matches the search, across all pages, before you remove them.

## Finding paths when your network is large

When you look for paths to a funder, the AI is shown people from your Network. One look considers at most 150 people.

- If you have 150 people or fewer, all of them are considered.
- If you have more, the system chooses whom to show the AI by a fixed rule it states every time: a person is chosen when a distinctive word in their name, affiliation, how you know them or notes also appears in a fact about the funder that you have approved. A "distinctive" word has at least 4 letters and is not a common word such as church, foundation, ministry, director or board. People who share more words come first, and a person whose whole name appears in a funder fact comes before the rest. If more than 150 people share a word, the 150 who share the most are considered.

Every result tells you how many people were considered out of how many you have recorded, and by what rule. "No paths" always means "none found among those considered". It never means no path exists: a person who shares no word with the funder's approved facts was not looked at, which is not a judgement about them.

If nobody on your Network shares a word with the funder's approved facts, the AI is not asked at all and nothing is charged. To widen the look, approve more of the funder's facts on the Research tab, or add where-they-work and how-you-know-them detail to the people you have recorded.

Nothing here ever contacts anyone on your list.
